Tips that worked in practice:
- Put the date as X prints it, e.g. `site:x.com "Sep 26, 2026" hiring engineer`. Keep queries short.
- Generic date queries saturate fast. Rotate in company names from your funding sweep and founder handles.
- Stop after ~10 calls if nothing new turns up.
